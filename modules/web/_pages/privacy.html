Shrublab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

About Shrublab

Shrublab is a privately operated collection of services available by invitation. This policy explains how those services handle personal information, including information from connected Google accounts. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact support@shrublab.xyz.

Connected Google accounts

Connecting a Google account is optional. We request the permissions needed for the integration you choose to use. Your Google email address and profile information identify the account connected to the service.

The current Gmail integration is Inbox Zero. Its permissions allow it to read email messages and metadata, organise messages and labels, compose and send messages, and manage Gmail settings and filters. It uses those permissions to provide the email-management features and automation you enable. Configured automation may perform actions on your behalf without a separate confirmation for each message.

Other Shrublab services do not receive access to your Gmail account merely because you use them. If a new integration needs additional Google permissions, it will request the corresponding authorisation.

AI processing

When AI-assisted email features are enabled, relevant email content is sent to external AI providers to perform the requested task, such as classification, summarisation or drafting. Classification uses Perplexity Decider through Vercel AI Gateway. Other AI tasks, and classification when that service fails, use Shrublab’s AI gateway to reach DeepSeek through Merge Gateway.

Google user data is not used to train or improve AI or machine learning models. Providers may retain submitted content under their applicable service terms; zero data retention is not guaranteed. Access and use must remain limited to delivering the requested features and other purposes permitted by Google’s Limited Use requirements, such as security investigations.

See the processing and retention terms for Vercel AI Gateway and Perplexity’s API. Other AI processing uses Merge Gateway and its selected DeepSeek endpoint. Provider processing and retention are separate from the application and backup retention described below.

Storage and service providers

Connection credentials, application settings and data needed by enabled features are stored on infrastructure used to operate Shrublab. Hosting, network and backup providers process information as needed to deliver those services. Access is restricted to authorised users and to the operator as needed for administration, support and security.

Operational logs may record information needed to troubleshoot failures and protect the services. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. It is shared only as needed to provide the features described in this policy, with your authorisation, or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

Connection credentials and application data are retained while needed to operate the integration. To request deletion of your connection and associated application data, contact support@shrublab.xyz. Deleted data may remain in access-controlled backups until those backups expire under their retention schedule; backups are not rewritten immediately after each deletion.

You can revoke Shrublab’s Google access through your Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents further authorised access to Google; it does not necessarily delete information already stored by Shrublab. Contact us if you also want that information deleted.

Cookies and sessions

Shrublab services use cookies or similar session tokens to keep you signed in and support account security. These are not used for advertising.

Google API data

Shrublab’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when integrations or data-handling practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
